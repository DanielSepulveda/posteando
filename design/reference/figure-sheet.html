How they are built

Four rules make any new figure belong to the family, whoever draws it.

  1. One grid, one flat colourEvery body fits a 100 by 100 square with the same margins. No outline, no gradient, no shading. The shape alone must be readable as its object.
  2. The eyes are the only faceSame two eyes on every figure: same size, same gap, same pupils. They sit on a line between 42% and 58% of the height, wherever the body is widest. No mouth, no brows, no arms.
  3. Corners from one familyRounding comes from three radii (small, medium, large). That is what makes a note and a bubble feel like siblings.
  4. A small cut for small sizesAt 24px and below the eyes grow a little so they stay readable, and fine details such as the fold on Nota drop out. Same idea, fewer details.

Six ways to feel

All expression comes from the eyes, so every figure can make every face. Six is the whole set. Each one is tied to a state of the product, not to decoration.

Motion is now quieter. Only the figure nearest your cursor glances at it, and the rest keep their own gaze. One figure blinks at a time, quickly and at uneven intervals, each eye closing on its own line.

Their inks

Each figure owns one ink, like the pens on a desk. All five are settled: cherry for Sello, pen blue for Copy, sticky-note yellow for Revisión, ledger green for Números and noir for the assistant. Yellow is the one light body, so its eyes turn dark.

The colours you sent are brighter than our cotton and cherry. I took that as a cue for the helpers, which are small and can carry more colour, and kept the base palette as it is. Paper stays quiet so the ink can be loud.

Where they do not go

Errors, billing and anything about data

No figure reacts to a failed post or a charge. Alerta exists for attention, not for comedy.

Below 16px, or crowded together

Under 16px the eyes close up. More than one figure in a view only in a line-up like the top of this page.

As decoration

A figure is there because someone is speaking or something happened. If it is only filling a corner, it goes.

With extra features

No mouths, hats, arms, seasonal costumes or new colours per campaign. The restraint is what keeps them from reading as childish.
