# Sheet

The post detail: a panel that slides in from the right over the view, so the calendar or table stays where it was.

- `sheet-width` wide on `surface-raised`, an ink line on its left and `shadow-sheet`. Full screen under `bp-drawer`.
- Its header is exactly `bar-height` and its second band exactly `subbar-height`, so its borders continue the view's borders.
- Header: brand monogram, mono crumb, `sheet-title`, the stage pill, close. Second band: the Stepper. Body: text, publications per account, approval record, review notes, history. Footer on `surface-page`: what happens next in words, then ghost actions and the one primary button.
- The approval record carries the Stamp. It is the view's one touch of character.
- It arrives in `dur-slide` with `ease-slide`. Escape and the close button dismiss it.
