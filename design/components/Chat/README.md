# Chat

A conversation with one helper. The helper speaks through its figure; you speak in noir.

- One helper per chat, and chats are private to the person. The helper's figure sits beside its bubbles and shows what it is doing through its eyes: pensando while working, contento when it delivers, alerta when it needs you.
- Helper bubbles are `surface-raised` with the ink line; yours are noir with white text, on the right.
- Anything the helper proposes is an `option` card with actions on it: one small primary ("Usar este") and tools for the common follow-ups. The person never has to copy and paste.
- The composer's placeholder names what this helper does. Each helper does only its job and says so plainly when asked for anything else.
- Chat is the one place where a figure appears at every turn.
