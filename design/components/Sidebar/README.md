# Sidebar

The sidebar says where you are: the wordmark, the brand picker, search, the sections, the helpers and their recent chats, credits, and you. The top bar says what you can do here.

- `sidebar-width` on `surface-desk`, one ink line on its right edge. Between `bp-drawer` and `bp-rail`, or when collapsed by hand, it becomes the icon rail (`rail-width`); under `bp-drawer` it is a drawer.
- The current section is a card with the ink line and `shadow-nav`. Hover is `surface-hover`. Nothing in the sidebar is a pastel or cherry except counts that need you (`count hot`) and unread dots.
- The brand picker sits at the top and filters the whole app. Brands are never listed as nav items: an agency can have fifty.
- Chats under Ayudantes show the helper's small figure and a cherry dot when unread; one helper per chat.
- Section names are plain nouns: Inicio, Calendario, Posts, Marcas, Biblioteca, Números.
