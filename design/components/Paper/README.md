# Paper

The website's desk: cuadrícula paper, cards that lie on it with a hard shadow, a tilted tag, a strip of masking tape. This is the full dose of the brand and it belongs to the website.

- Ground is `surface-desk`; a `paper` area is `surface-page` with `line-grid` lines at `grid-pitch`.
- A `desk-card` uses `radius-xl` and `shadow-desk`, and may tilt between 1 and 3 degrees. Tilt objects, never text blocks or anything the person must read at length.
- Tape is `marker-lavender` at 82% with dotted ends, tilted 4 degrees, holding the top of one object per section.
- Figures live at the edges of objects, peeking from behind them, never in the middle of a layout.
- Every object must pass the desk test: would this exist on a papelería desk? Paper, tape, stamps, receipts, sticky notes and highlighters pass. Glass, glows, blurs and gradients do not.
- None of this goes into the app shell. The app keeps the ink line, the highlighters and one touch of character per view.
