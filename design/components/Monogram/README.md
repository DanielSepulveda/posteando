# Monogram

Brands are square noir-outlined monograms (two letters in mono); people are round noir avatars with one letter.

- Brands never get a colour of their own. Colour means state, so a brand is told apart by its letters and name.
- Square is a brand, round is a person. Do not mix the two.
- Sizes: 18, 22 (default) and 34 for monograms; 22 and 28 for avatars.
- When a brand has uploaded a real logo, it sits inside the same square with the same line.
